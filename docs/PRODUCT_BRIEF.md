# Product Brief: Marketplace Audit OS

## Overview
Marketplace Audit OS is a multi-tenant B2B SaaS platform designed for agencies, consultants, brands, and marketplace teams to audit and improve Shopee and TikTok Shop stores. 

## Target Audience
- **Agencies**: Manage multiple clients, brands, and stores.
- **Consultants**: Perform audits and generate recommendations.
- **Brands**: Oversee their presence across multiple marketplace platforms.
- **Marketplace Teams**: Analyze performance and enforce standards.

## Core Value Proposition
Provide a centralized, secure workspace to manage clients, track stores, perform standardized audits, score marketplace presence, and generate actionable recommendations.

## Future Vision (Beyond Sprint 0)
- Advanced audit templates and checklists
- Automated marketplace scoring
- Findings, recommendations, and task management
- Analytics and report generation
- Subscription billing
